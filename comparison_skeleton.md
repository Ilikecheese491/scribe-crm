# CRM Comparison Page: Scribe vs Competitors

**Pattern:** Feature-by-feature with SMB-specific angles  
**Primary wedge:** "Runs itself" — lead with setup time/automation, not feature parity

---

## Pillar Context

### The SMB Satisfaction Gap
- **37% of SMBs are satisfied** with their current CRM (Gartner 2024)
- **70% of SMB CRM projects fail** to deliver lasting value
- **Market size:** $86.4B in 2026 (Grand View Research), 9.3% CAGR
- **Key pain point:** Manual data entry + complexity = 8+ hours/week wasted

### Why "Runs Itself" Matters
SMBs don't need more features—they need less work. The three failure patterns:
1. **Too complicated** — 70% abandon CRMs within 6 months
2. **Manual work never ends** — 32% lack technical expertise for automation
3. **Unused features** — 43% pay for 50+ features, use only 30%

## Competitor-Specific Pages

### Page 1: HubSpot vs Scribe

**HubSpot Pricing:** Free tier for 2 users. Paid tiers start at $50/month ($50/seat for 2 users, drops to $45 at 11+ seats). CRM-only Starter is $20/month.

| Criteria | Scribe | HubSpot | Our Advantage |
|----------|--------|---------|---------------|
| **Setup Time** | 5 minutes | 2+ hours | Zero-configuration |
| **First Value** | Immediate | 1+ week | Auto-sync from day 1 |
| **Pricing (3 users)** | Free + $27/mo | $20/mo | No credit card |
| **Free Tier** | 3 users full | 2 users limited | More usable free tier |
| **Automation** | Built-in "runs itself" | Bolted-on AI agents | Native automation |
| **Learning Curve** | Minimal | Steep | Designed for non-tech users |

**HubSpot's Positioning Weakness:** Marketed as "CRM platform" — enterprises build workflows, SMBs need them pre-built.

## Vertical Variant Pages

### Section 3: CRM for Service Businesses

**Key Features:** Booking/appointment sync, client communication automation, invoice tracking, project management, service delivery tracking.

**Comparison Focus:**
- Scribe: Integrates with Calendly, Stripe, QuickBooks natively
- HubSpot: Requires Zapier or integrations for full service stack
- Zoho: Separate modules (Books, Projects, Desk) — complex setup
- Pipedrive: No native service/project features
- Close: No service/business features

---

### Section 4: Real Estate CRM

**Key Features:** Lead capture from listing sites (Zillow, Realtor.com), showing scheduler automation, commission tracking, transaction management.

**Comparison Focus:**
- Scribe: Auto-imports listings, syncs showing calendars, tracks commissions by transaction
- HubSpot: Real estate templates but requires setup
- Zoho: Separate real-estate module, technical setup
- Pipedrive: Pipeline-focused, limited transaction tools
- Close: No listing site integrations

---

### Section 5: Healthcare / Professional Services

**Key Features:** HIPAA-compliant notes (where applicable), appointment reminders, client follow-up sequences, treatment tracking, billing sync.

**Comparison Focus:**
- Scribe: Appointment reminders via SMS/email, client follow-up automation
- HubSpot: Requires compliance setup, enterprise-level features
- Zoho: Healthcare-specific module, complex configuration
- Pipedrive: No healthcare features
- Close: No healthcare features
