# SMB CRM Channel Execution Plan

**Launch Strategy:** Organic-first 30 days  
**Paid Spend Gate:** Day-30 = 150+ qualified trials AND ≥25% activation

---

## Organic Channels (Days 1-30)

### Week 1: Foundation
- **GitHub Pages/Netlify site live** — pillar page
- **Product Hunt** — soft announce to tech SMBs
- **LinkedIn Company Page** — 3 founder posts (problem, solution, team)

### Week 2: Content Distribution
- **Reddit** — r/smallbusiness, r/entrepreneur (value-first)
- **Hacker News** — "Show HN: A CRM that runs itself" (if traction)
- **Twitter Threads** — daily pain point → Scribe solution

### Week 3: Community Building
- **Facebook Groups** — SMB owners, service business owners
- **Quora** — answer CRM questions, link to pillar
- **Email Sequences** — outbound to waitlist (if any)

### Week 4: Review Sites
- **G2 Crowd** — early reviewer program
- **Capterra** — listing request
- **Trustpilot** — verified customer reviews

---

## Partnerships (Ongoing)

| Partner | Value | Metric |
|---------|-------|--------|
| SaaS tool directories | SEO backlinks | Domain authority lift |
| Small biz podcasts | Guest appearances | Brand awareness |
| Industry newsletters | Sponsorship | CTR to pricing |

---

## Paid Spend Gate (Post Day-30)

**Trigger:** 150+ qualified trials AND ≥25% activation rate

**Budget allocation if triggered:**
- 40% Google Ads (brand + competitor keywords)
- 30% LinkedIn Ads (service businesses)
- 20% Reddit Ads (small biz communities)
- 10% Referral program (if viral loop works)
