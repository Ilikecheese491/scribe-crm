## The Problem: Why CRM Setup Fails Small Businesses

You’re not doing anything wrong. The data is clear: **55% of CRM projects fail expectations** within the first six months—not because you’re incompetent, but because the products themselves are built for enterprises, not small businesses.

### The Three Patterns We See Everywhere

1. **Too complicated** — 43% pay for 50x features but use only 30%; 55% fail expectations within six months because entering data takes longer than it saves.  
2. **Manual work never ends** — 32% lack the technical expertise to configure automation flows properly.  
3. **Unused features** — 43% of SMBs pay for 50+ features but only use 30% of them.

You end up with spreadsheets that never sync, follow-ups that never happen, and deals that fall through the cracks. The average small business owner spends **8 hours per week** on manual CRM tasks—time that could be growing your business instead.

That’s your opening. The gap isn’t lack of demand—it’s that **existing solutions don’t actually run themselves**. They require constant babysitting, data entry, and workflow management from people who don’t have time for that.
