## Core Promise

> "Set up your CRM once. It runs itself forever."

**Why it works:**
- Positions as automation-first, not generic CRM wrapper
- Addresses SMB pain: "I don't have time to learn complicated software"
- Whitespace: No competitor owns "runs itself" language
- AI angle: Automation without the complexity SMBs can't afford

---

## The Problem (SMB Failure Patterns)

**SMB CRM Dissatisfaction Data:**
- **55% of CRM implementations fail to meet expectations** (Gartner cited by CustomerThink)
- **43% use less than half CRM features** (HubSpot State of Marketing 2025)
- **32% lack technical expertise** (SMB Trends 2024 Report)
- **37% SMB satisfaction rate** (Gartner CRM Satisfaction Study 2024)

These are not product issues—they are workflow mismatches. SMBs pick powerful tools but lack time to configure them properly. The solution is not more features; it's automation that works from day one.

## Key Objections & Responses

### "Is a free CRM good enough?"
**Response:** "Free is not the promise. Automation is. We give you both—start free, upgrade only when you need more."

### "Won't I need setup help?"
**Response:** "If setup takes more than 5 minutes, we've failed. Our onboarding is a 3-minute video."

### "Where is your data? Are you reliable?"
**Response:** "Production-ready infrastructure with daily backups. We are not live yet, so no uptime variance to cite."
