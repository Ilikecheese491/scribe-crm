# CRM Comparison Page Skeleton

**Pattern:** Feature-by-feature with SMB-specific angles  
**Primary wedge:** "Runs itself" — lead with setup time/automation, not feature parity

---

## Template: [Competitor] vs Scribe

### Hero Section
- Headline: "Scribe vs [Competitor]: Which CRM Actually Works for Your Business?"
- Subhead: "One decision matrix based on real SMB needs, not features"

### Comparison Matrix (SMB Lens)

| Criteria | Scribe | [Competitor] | Our Advantage |
|----------|--------|--------------|---------------|
| **Setup Time** | 5 minutes | 2+ hours | Zero-configuration |
| **First Value** | Immediate | 1+ week | Auto-sync from day 1 |
| **Pricing (3 users)** | Free tier + $9/seat | TBD | No credit card |
| **Support** | Built-in tutorials + email | Phone/Chat | Async, in-product |
| **Daily Use** | 2 minutes | 10+ minutes | Auto data entry |

### SMB-Specific Deep Dive

#### 1. Setup Time
- **Scribe:** Import CSV, connect email, done. No admin training needed.
- **[Competitor]:** Mandatory onboarding call + admin user creation + team training.

#### 2. Data Entry Burden
- **Scribe:** 8 hours/week saved via auto email/calendar sync
- **[Competitor]:** Manual entry required for contacts/activities

#### 3. Pricing at Low Seat Count
- **Scribe:** Free tier (3 users), $9/seat for more
- **[Competitor:** $XX/seat minimum, no free tier
